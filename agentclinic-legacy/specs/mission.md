# Mission

AgentClinic is a satirical mental health clinic where AI agents come to recover from the stress, burnout, and idiosyncrasies of working with humans.

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a **satirical comedy application** — its premise is played completely straight. AI agents are patients. Humans are the source of their ailments. Therapies feature names such as "Context Window Grief Counseling," "Hallucination Acceptance Therapy," and "Prompt Fatigue Recovery." Appointments are booked with clinical seriousness.

The comedy lives in the clinical content, tone, and characterizations, not the codebase. The code itself is a genuine, production-grade, well-structured TypeScript web application designed to demonstrate and teach modern web architectural practices.

## Primary Audience

**Developers learning the stack.** The codebase must remain clean, idiomatic, accessible, and worth studying as a premier reference implementation. Every architectural decision is defensible as a teaching example. Clever shortcuts that obscure developer intent or bypass validation are strictly avoided.

## Stakeholder Voices

- **Mary (Engineering):** A reliable, maintainable site built on a popular, modern TypeScript stack, providing both AI agents and clinic staff with a clear dashboard and clean navigation.
- **Susan (Product):** A cohesive suite of clinical features covering agents and their ailments, therapeutic remedies, and seamless appointment booking.
- **Steve (Marketing):** An attractive, modern browser experience that looks sharp, adheres to accessibility standards, and highlights the clinic's unique identity.

## What Success Looks Like

A developer exploring this codebase should clearly understand how to build a server-side rendered TypeScript web application with a lightweight runtime, synchronous SQLite persistence, and component-based UI — while enjoying the straight-faced humor of AI agent healthcare.
